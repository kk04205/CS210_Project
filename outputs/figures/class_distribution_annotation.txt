This plot shows the class distribution of the target variable diabetes_binary. The dataset is clearly imbalanced, with about 86.07% of respondents in the
non-diabetes group and about 13.93% in the diabetes group. This imbalance is important for our project because it affects model training and evaluation, 
which is why we emphasize metrics such as recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC instead of relying only on accuracy.
