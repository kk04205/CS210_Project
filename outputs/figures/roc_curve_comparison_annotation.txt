This ROC curve plot compares the discrimination ability of all models across decision thresholds. Gradient Boosting achieves the highest ROC-AUC at 0.827, 
while Logistic Regression and SVM both perform strongly at 0.818. These curves show that the best-performing models can separate diabetic and non-diabetic 
respondents much better than random guessing. The figure is important for our project because ROC-AUC provides a threshold-independent measure of predictive 
quality and helps us compare models more fairly on an imbalanced dataset.
