This heatmap visualizes pairwise correlations among the health and demographic variables used in the project. Most correlations are moderate rather than extremely strong,
which suggests that the dataset contains multiple complementary predictors rather than a few highly redundant ones. The plot also shows that variables such as BMI, age, 
high blood pressure, general health, and difficulty walking have more noticeable relationships with diabetes status, which aligns with our goal of understanding how different 
health indicators contribute to risk classification.
