This chart compares diabetes rates across several key binary health indicators, including high blood pressure, high cholesterol, smoking status, physical activity, 
heart disease or attack history, and difficulty walking. In general, respondents with adverse health conditions such as high blood pressure, heart disease history, 
and difficulty walking show much higher diabetes rates, highlighting that these variables are strongly associated with diabetes risk. This figure directly supports 
our project’s aim of linking survey-based health indicators to meaningful and interpretable risk patterns.
